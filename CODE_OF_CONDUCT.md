# Code of conduct

This is a small team building software that a shopkeeper depends on for their statutory
filings. Two commitments:

**To each other.** Assume competence. Disagree about the work, never about the person.
No one is expected to be available outside their committed hours.

**To the shop.** Pilot staff and the owner are colleagues, not test subjects. Their time at
the counter is revenue; keep sessions short, arrive prepared, and never leave the shop
unable to serve a customer because of something we changed.

Raise a concern with the sponsor, Annachi Kadai Co, directly. Anything raised is handled in
confidence.
